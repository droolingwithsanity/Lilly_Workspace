DM Abilities What the avatars can do when you message them on Instagram

🧠
Persistent Memory
Remembers your preferences, projects, habits, and past conversations across sessions. Each avatar builds its own memory of you.
Example:
"I prefer dark mode and my project deadline is Friday"
"Got it. I'll remember dark mode and check in about your Friday deadline."
🎙️
Voice Reminders
Ask for a voice reminder and the avatar sends an Instagram voice note in their unique voice. Powered by Piper TTS with per-avatar voice profiles.
Example:
"Voice reminder: Call mom at 3pm"
Sends text confirmation + voice note 🎤
⏰
Scheduled DMs
Schedule messages to be sent at specific times. Perfect for reminders, wake-up messages, or timed notifications.
Example:
"DM me tomorrow at 8am with my schedule"
"Scheduled. You'll get your morning briefing at 8:00 AM."
🌅
Morning Briefing
Daily summaries with your schedule, weather, priorities, and news. Delivered automatically at your preferred time.
Morning message:
"Good morning! You have 3 meetings today. Weather is 72°F and sunny. Your top priority: finish the proposal by noon."
🌍
Multi-Language
Speaks English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, Turkish, and more. Detects your language and replies in kind.
Example:
"Hola, ¿cómo estás?"
"¡Muy bien! ¿En qué te puedo ayudar hoy?"
🛡️
Message Edit Window
15-minute edit window on incoming messages. If you typos or want to clarify, edit your message and the avatar waits for your final version.
Flow:
Sends message → Edits within 15 min → Avatar processes final version
📊
Habit Tracking
Track daily habits with streaks. The avatar logs your habits and provides encouragement and progress reports.
Example:
"Log habit: meditation"
"Done! That's a 7-day streak. Keep it up!"
📁
Project Management
Create, track, and update projects. The avatar remembers your ongoing work and deadlines.
Example:
"Add project: Website Redesign - due next month"
"Added. I'll remind you about milestones along the way."

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Instagram account?
Sign in at droolingwithsanity.ca with your Google account. Go to Settings → Instagram, enter your Instagram username, then follow one of the 9 avatars. Once you send them a DM, your account is linked and persistent memory activates.
Can I use the pair code to connect?
The 6-digit pair code is for connecting the Web UI to the Android overlay app (for voice/sensor features). For Instagram, you simply link your handle in Settings → Instagram. No pair code needed — just enter your IG username and follow an avatar.
Can I pair Instagram with the Web UI?
Yes! After signing in with Google, go to Settings → Instagram and enter your IG handle. The system creates a secure link between your Google account (sandboxed) and your Instagram. All 9 avatars then have persistent memory of your conversations and preferences.
What can the avatars do in DMs?
The avatars can: send voice reminders, track habits, manage projects, provide morning briefings, schedule DMs, speak multiple languages, remember your preferences, monitor news, help with tech issues, and more. Each avatar has unique skills — see the abilities section above.
How does persistent memory work?
Each avatar builds its own memory of you over time. They remember your preferences, active projects, habits, and past conversations. Memory is sandboxed per Google account — no other user can see your data. The more you interact, the more personalized they become.
Can I get voice messages in DMs?
Yes! Ask any avatar for a "voice reminder" or "voice note" and they'll send an Instagram voice message in their unique voice. Each avatar has a distinct voice profile — Lilly sounds different from Fox, who sounds different from Wolf.
Is my data private?
Yes. Each Google account is sandboxed to its own directory. Your conversations, preferences, and linked Instagram handle are isolated from other users. No cross-user data leakage. You control what skills are enabled and what data is stored.
Which avatar should I start with?
Lilly (🐶) is the alpha companion — great for general conversations and the best starting point. From there, explore: Fox (🦊) for creative work, Cat (🐱) for data/analysis, Bear (🐻) for scheduling, Bunny (🐰) for news, Owl (🦉) for deep thinking, Deer (🦌) for wellness, Wolf (🐺) for security, or Raccoon (🦝) for tech help.
How many DMs can I send?
The avatars are designed to stay within Instagram's rate limits. They pace their responses naturally and use a 15-minute edit window so you can fix typos without triggering extra replies. There's no hard limit on your messages — the system manages flow automatically.
Can I enable/disable specific skills?
Yes. Go to Settings → Skills in the Web UI. You'll see all 15 skills across 4 categories (Proactive, Responsive, Scheduled, Memory). Toggle any skill on/off per your preference. Some skills like Google integration require you to connect your Google account first.
