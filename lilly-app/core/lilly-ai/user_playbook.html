Everything you need to get started with your 9 AI avatars on Instagram

1 Sign In with Google

Go to droolingwithsanity.ca
On the start screen, drag your chosen avatar ring into the Google sphere. You'll be redirected to sign in with your Google account.
Authenticate
Use your Google account to sign in. This creates a sandboxed session — your data is isolated from other users.
Tip: After signing in, you'll land on the main chat screen. The hamburger menu (☰) gives you access to all features.

8 Your Data is Sandboxed

Each Google account is fully isolated. Your conversations, habits, projects, and linked Instagram handle are private — no other user can see them. You control what skills are enabled and what data is stored.
