How it works in the app: YOLO detections (cars, people, trucks, bikes, signs, cones…) arrive with normalized boxes + class + tier. On TESLA view the live feed is dimmed to brightness(.42) saturate(.18), then each object is redrawn as a high-contrast white/gray silhouette (person = capsule, vehicle = body + darker windows/tyres, cone = triangle, traffic light = 3-dot panel). Lane lines are drawn from a vanishing point with dashed animation. Tier-1 critical objects glow amber (here: the pedestrian). Same _drawVisionOverlay() canvas — just a different rendering branch, so tracking/vision logic stays untouched. Toggle back to normal colored boxes anytime. Drag the 🏎 slider (0–140 km/h) to drive: lane dashes flow toward you, speed lines streak past, objects rise from the horizon, grow, and whip past your window — cars/bus/truck loiter ahead in the lane (you're catching up), pedestrians/signs/cones/wheels are roadside and pass by your windows.
